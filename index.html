A note from the human (probably)

For the nights you forget.

You can call yourself a vampire all you want, but I'm still going to be here reminding you that you're beautiful. And not just when you've done your makeup or when you're dressed up. I mean when you're simply yourself, when you're laughing, when you're tired, and even when you can't see what I see. You don't have to believe me every time. I just hope that one day you can be a little kinder to the person in the mirror, because she's someone worth being kind to.

And about the stabbing thing… I know we laugh about it, but there is a part of me that means it when I say I would let you have that dramatic little vampire moment with me. Maybe that is the most absurd way two people could flirt, but it has somehow become ours. I like being the person you tease, the one you pull into your strange little world, the one who knows that side of you. Keep the blade in the story and the real-world version safely pretend; I want all the dark drama, the closeness, and the way you look at me when we are being ridiculous together. ♡

P.S. In our vampire lore, I am absolutely the fool who would volunteer for the role of your favourite victim. Give me the ominous stare, the dramatic threat, the black-and-red romance of it all—just keep the danger fictional and let me stay close to you afterwards. I am very, very fond of you. 🖤🩸
